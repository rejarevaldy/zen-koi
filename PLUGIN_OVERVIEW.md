## A koi pond while your agent works

When an agent starts a turn, a pixel-art koi pond opens on top of the thread's chat input. Koi swim slowly, rest, and drift deeper and back up. There is nothing to win or lose. Click the water to scatter a little food and the nearest koi come to eat.

## Breathe with the pond

A small guide paces your breathing at five breaths a minute, with a longer out-breath, and each in-breath sends one slow ring across the water. Turn it off for short reminders to rest instead.

## Seasons and light

Spring petals, summer fireflies, autumn leaves, and winter snow, with water and light that follow your clock. A stone lantern lights up from dusk to dawn. While the pond is open, pale water rises behind the text in the chat input.

Nothing is stored on the server. Clicking the pond never takes focus from the chat input, and the pond honors reduced-motion settings.
