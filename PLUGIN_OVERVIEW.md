## A koi pond while your agent works

When an agent starts a turn, a pixel-art koi pond opens on top of the thread's chat input and runs on into it, so your koi swim under the text you type. Koi wander, rest, keep each other company, and come over when your pointer rests on the water. Click to scatter a little food.

## Koi-sukui

Switch to koi-sukui, the festival game of scooping fish with a paper poi, played gently. Dip slowly and lift soon: a splashy entry tears the paper, koi flee a poi swept through the water, and only koi near the surface can be lifted. Every koi you scoop rests in a wooden bowl, then goes back. Leave the poi alone and it plays by itself.

## Your own pond

Open the Zen Pond page from the navigation rail to visit any time. Add, name, and release up to nine koi from ten varieties, and watch them grow over two weeks. Choose a stone lantern, an overhanging tree that changes with the seasons, lily pads, and water plants, and set the season, time of day, rain, sound, and a breathing guide.

Sound is off until you turn it on: water, a wind chime, and a shishi-odoshi. The pond honors reduced-motion settings and never takes focus from the chat input.
